# AI at work: four ways it turns on us

## intro {hold=1}
What happens when our own staff, and our own assistants, use AI? [#four] It can turn on us in four ways.

## leak
First, our secrets go in and do not come back. [#paste] A colleague puts a contract of forty pages into a free chatbot for a summary. [#keep] Free tools typically keep what is typed, and may use it to train the next model. [#nolog] There is no alert and no log. [#ban] Forbidding the tools does not work. The paste moves to a personal phone.

## hijack
Second, our assistant takes orders from a stranger. [#mail] An attacker sends one email with instructions hidden in white text. [#obeys] The assistant cannot tell our instructions from text it happens to read. [#rights] The more it may send, delete or pay, the more one email can do.

## deepfake
Third, the {CFO|C F O} on the phone is not the {CFO|C F O}. [#clone] A few minutes of public audio is enough to clone a voice. [#fri] The attacker phones Finance late on a Friday, and urgency is the weapon. [#proof] Voice and video can no longer prove who someone is.

## made
Fourth, it sounded right, and it was made up. [#fluent] AI is wrong in exactly the same tone as when it is right. [#ruling] A memo reaches the board table with a court ruling that does not exist. [#draft] The output is a draft, like a junior colleague's.

## good {hold=1.2}
What good looks like. [#tool] One approved tool, with no training on our data. [#least] {AI|A I} agents get the access of the task, and a person approves anything that sends, deletes or pays. [#call] Requests for money by voice are confirmed on a known number. [#owasp] {OWASP|oh wasp} ranks prompt injection first in its {LLM|L L M} Top 10. [#act] And the {AI|A I} Act, Article 4, asks us to support staff's {AI|A I} literacy.

## first {hold=1.2}
A suggested first step. [#inv] Start with an inventory of where AI is used, with what data and what rights. [#one] Then consider one approved tool and a usage policy of one page. [#call] Where to start is your call.

## outro {hold=2}
Four ways AI turns on us. [#draft] Treat the output as a draft, and everything it reads as untrusted.
