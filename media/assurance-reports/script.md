# The 90-page report: what a clean opinion does and does not tell you

## intro {hold=1}
A supplier sends a {SOC 2|sock two} report, and someone files it as audited. [#four] Four pages decide whether that means anything.

## type
First, the type and the period. [#one] A Type 1 report covers one day. [#two] A Type 2 report covers a period, and it ended months ago. [#six] The standard says a Type 2 ordinarily covers at least six months. [#photo] The report is a photograph of the past, not a guarantee for next year.

## scope
Second, the scope. [#line] The opinion covers exactly what the system description says. [#module] Your module or region may not be in it. [#carve] A cloud provider may be carved out, and so not tested. [#none] A clean opinion on the wrong scope is no assurance.

## part
Third, your part. [#cuec] Every report lists the controls the supplier assumed you run. [#untested] The auditor did not test them. [#names] Typical lines: who gets access, who removes leavers, who reviews the logs. [#owner] They rarely reach the people who run our side.

## exceptions
Fourth, the exceptions at the back. [#p3] The opinion is on page three. [#p60] The exceptions are on page sixty. [#normal] A clean opinion with exceptions is normal. [#read] Read each one for what it means for our service. [#never] And configuration, access and data use are never tested.

## good {hold=1.2}
What good looks like. [#four2] Type, period, date and auditor are recorded before anyone reads on. [#map] A scope map has one line per service: in, carved out, or not covered. [#list] Every assumed control gets a named owner. [#review] Every exception gets a rating for our service. [#cost] This takes an afternoon per report. [#nis2] {NIS2|niss two} requires supply chain security measures, in Article 21, and the report is one way to show them.

## first {hold=1.2}
A suggested first step. [#ask] Is our service in scope, in a Type 2, less than a year old? [#who] Who owns our list of assumed controls? [#decide] Accepting a weaker report can be reasonable, as long as it is a decision.

## outro {hold=2}
Four pages. [#keep] Read the report for what it covers, and what it leaves to us.
