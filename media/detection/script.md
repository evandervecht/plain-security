# Would we even notice? Four ways an attack stays invisible

## intro {hold=1}
Would we even notice an attack? [#four] Four ways it stays invisible.

## logs
First, the evidence was deleted before anyone looked. [#median] Breaches often go unnoticed for weeks. Mandiant reports a global median of fourteen days, in its 2026 report. [#overwrite] If logs were never kept, or were erased after two weeks, the answer is "we do not know". [#max] Then every customer record becomes possibly affected.

## alarm
Second, the alarm went off. Nobody was listening. [#tool] A monitoring tool produces alerts, not attention. [#night] The one that mattered, a new administrator at {02:40|two forty} on a Saturday, is read on Monday. [#weekend] A weekend is enough to encrypt everything.

## blind
Third, we watch where it is easy. [#cloud] Cloud accounts, software tools and old controllers are often missing. [#half] Ask for the coverage map, and the honest answer is often "we see about half". [#map] The first step is a single page. Per system, watched or not, and who signed off on the "not".

## response
Fourth, detected on Friday, handled on Monday. [#queue] The alert becomes a ticket and waits. [#perm] Nobody is sure they may pull a server off the network on a Friday evening. [#decide] Two decisions for this room: someone on duty, and written permission to act without asking.

## good {hold=1.2}
What good looks like. [#logs] Central logs that an attacker cannot edit, kept for at least a year. [#ten] Ten written detections that matter, with someone watching around the clock. [#map2] A signed coverage map. [#play] Playbooks, with standing authority to isolate a machine. [#nis2] {NIS2|N I S two} Article 21 names incident handling, and you cannot report within {24 hours|twenty four hours} what you cannot detect.

## first {hold=1.2}
A suggested first step. [#ask] At the next meeting, ask one thing. [#mttd] Last quarter, how long on average to detect an attack? [#none] If nobody can answer, that is the answer.

## outro {hold=2}
Four ways an attack stays invisible. [#keep] Measure how fast we notice.
