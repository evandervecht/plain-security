# We sell products with software in them. What does the CRA ask of us, and from when?

## intro {hold=1}
Do we sell anything that connects to something? [#cra] The Cyber Resilience Act is EU law for makers of connected hardware and software. [#four] Four questions decide what it means for us.

## maker
First, are we a maker? [#sell] The act covers hardware and software that connects to a device or a network, sold in the EU. [#brand] Selling under our own name makes us the manufacturer. [#class] Each product also has a class: default, class one, class two or critical.

## report
Second, the alarm. [#since] Since 11 September 2026, the CRA requires an early warning within 24 hours of becoming aware that a vulnerability is actively exploited. [#72] A notification follows within 72 hours. [#old] This also covers products we sold before December 2027. [#fine] Late reporting can be fined up to 15 million, or 2.5 percent of worldwide turnover.

## design
Third, from 11 December 2027. [#sec] Every new product needs security by design, [#sbom] an {SBOM|ess bomb}, [#sup] a support period with updates, [#ce] and a CE mark. [#no] Without the mark, no EU market.

## import
Fourth, importers and resellers. [#imp] An importer checks the manufacturer's file and the CE mark. [#res] A reseller verifies the mark, and passes on warnings. [#own] Put our own brand on a product, and we are the manufacturer.

## good {hold=1.2}
What good looks like. [#list] One product list, with a class for each. [#route] A reporting route, with one named person and a platform account. [#sbom2] An {SBOM|ess bomb} and a vulnerability process. [#cefile] A conformity file for the CE mark. [#annex] The essential requirements are in Annex One of the act.

## first {hold=1.2}
A suggested first step. [#ask] Ask for one product list with a class for each product. [#rehearse] Then rehearse one 24-hour report. [#sbomfirst] An {SBOM|ess bomb} and a vulnerability process are the quickest wins.

## outro {hold=2}
The act makes product security a condition for selling in the EU. [#keep] Know the list, and keep the clock.
