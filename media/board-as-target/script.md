# You are the target: four routes in

## intro {hold=1}
If an attacker wanted our company, would they start with you? [#four] The board is the target, by four routes.

## home
First, home. [#work] Your work laptop is protected with multi-factor authentication. [#private] Your private email, phone and family tablet are not. [#ncsc] The UK NCSC calls people in your position high-risk individuals, and says attackers prefer personal accounts because they are easier. [#set] Two free settings help: the strongest email protection, and Lockdown Mode on an iPhone.

## voice
Second, your voice and face are public. [#keynote] Every keynote and podcast gives a few minutes of audio. [#arup] In January 2024, an engineering firm's employee in Hong Kong paid {HK$200 million|200 million Hong Kong dollars} after a video call. Every other participant was a deepfake. [#nohack] No system was hacked. Only authority was used.

## pack
Third, the board pack. [#pdf] One PDF, sent to ten people every month, holds strategy, results and legal disputes. [#leak] The leak rarely comes from the portal. It comes from the copy forwarded to a private mailbox, printed for the train, or opened on the family tablet. [#privacy] With personal data, a stolen pack can mean a 72-hour notification.

## road
Fourth, the road. [#phone] On a trip, the phone is the company: mail, the board pack and banking. [#border] At a border it can be inspected. [#safe] In a hotel, the safe is opened by staff. [#aivd] The {AIVD|A I V D} advises a temporary phone and laptop for countries with an espionage risk.

## good {hold=1.2}
What good looks like. [#set2] Strongest protection on private mail, set up by the company. [#call] A call-back rule: no payment or bank change on a call or video alone. [#portal] The board pack is read in the portal, on a managed device. [#kit] A travel kit, with a loaner device for high-risk countries. [#ncsc2] The UK NCSC has written guidance for high-risk individuals.

## first {hold=1.2}
A suggested first step. [#ask] Ask the company to set up the strongest email protection for each household. [#rule] Then agree one rule: call back on a known number before any money moves.

## outro {hold=2}
You are the target, not only the company. [#keep] Most routes close with a setting, a rule or a kit.
