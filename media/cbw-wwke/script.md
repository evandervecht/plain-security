# Cbw and Wwke: one date, two kinds of resilience

## intro {hold=1}
Which Dutch law applies to us, and who in this room owns the answer? [#date] Two laws started on the same day, 15 August 2026.

## scope
First, which law applies. [#cbw] The {Cbw|C B W} covers your systems. It applies to about 8,000 organisations, and you assess yourself. [#wwke] The {Wwke|W W K E} covers buildings, people and recovery. It applies to about 500, and only after your sector minister designates you. [#link] A critical entity under the Wwke is an essential entity under the Cbw. The reverse does not hold.

## building
Second, the Wwke adds the building and the people. [#flood] It asks about floods, fire, sabotage and a supplier that stops delivering. [#months] After designation, it requires a risk assessment within nine months, and measures within ten. [#staff] Those measures include background checks for critical roles.

## clocks
Third, two clocks. [#wwke24] Under the Wwke, a significant disruption goes to the competent authority within 24 hours. [#cbw24] Under the Cbw, it is an early warning within 24 hours, a notification within 72 hours, and a final report within one month. [#fire] A fire that takes down a data hall can start both clocks at once.

## gap
Fourth, the gap between two programmes. [#two] Facilities, HR and IT can each assess half of the same site. [#cost] Two parallel programmes cost more, and leave the gap between them with no owner. [#one] We suggest one board decision instead.

## good {hold=1.2}
What good looks like. [#own] One executive owns resilience, cyber and physical. [#risk] One risk assessment per essential service. [#drill] One incident process, with two report templates. [#evid] One evidence file for both supervisors. [#law] The Cbw names the management body, and requires it to approve the measures and follow training.

## first {hold=1.2}
A suggested first step. [#scope] Ask legal to settle scope now, not after a designation letter arrives. [#sign] Then date and sign one decision that covers both laws.

## outro {hold=2}
The law does not care whether a hacker or a flood stopped the service. [#keep] Decide once, and name the owner.
