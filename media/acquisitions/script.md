# Buying a company, buying its breaches

## intro {hold=1}
We are buying a company. [#buy] What else are we buying? [#five] Five questions decide whether the deal brings value or an incident.

## inside
First, is an attacker already inside? [#sw] Marriott bought Starwood in 2016. [#in] According to the UK ICO, an attacker got into Starwood's systems in 2014. [#found] Marriott found it in September 2018. [#fine] The ICO fined Marriott {£18.4 million|eighteen point four million pounds} for not securing the systems it had acquired.

## estate
Second, what are we really buying? [#list] The target's asset list shows what its IT team runs. [#shadow] It rarely shows the agency website, the cloud account a developer opened on a company card, or the remote access box nobody patched. [#owner] What nobody listed, nobody patches.

## connect
Third, when do we connect? [#link] Linking networks on day one links their weaknesses to ours. [#bridge] We suggest a bridge period, with controlled gateways. [#after] Connect only after the compromise assessment, the admin password resets and MFA.

## paper
Fourth, who pays for a breach that started before closing? [#warr] A warranty of no known breach means little if nobody looked. [#indem] Consider a specific cyber indemnity. [#wi] {W&I|W and I} insurance typically excludes what was already known at signing.

## data
Fifth, which personal data comes with the deal? [#recs] Customer and staff records come with the promises made about them. [#clock] An old breach found after closing can still need reporting. Under {GDPR|G D P R} Article 33, the clock is 72 hours from becoming aware.

## good {hold=1.2}
What good looks like. [#look] A compromise assessment before we connect anything. [#inv] A full inventory with an owner for each system. {NIS2|niss two} Article 21 lists asset management. [#stage] A staged connection. [#ind] A cyber indemnity, and the insurer's answer in writing. [#dat] A data inventory before any systems merge.

## first {hold=1.2}
A suggested first step. [#ask] Ask who decides when the networks connect. [#cond] And on what conditions. [#ca] Ask for a compromise assessment first.

## outro {hold=2}
In a deal, security is part of the price. [#look2] Look before you connect.
