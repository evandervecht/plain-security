# NIS2 and DORA: the law now names the board

## intro {hold=1}
Who in this room is personally accountable if we are hit? [#two] Two European rules, NIS2 and {DORA|dora}, now name the board.

## board
First, the board is accountable. [#approve] NIS2 requires the management body to approve the cybersecurity risk measures and oversee them. [#liable] It can be held liable when it does not. [#nl] In the Netherlands, the Cbw has been in force since 15 August 2026. [#dora] DORA has applied to banks, insurers and investment firms since 17 January 2025. [#deleg] Delegating the work is allowed. Delegating the responsibility is not.

## clock
Second, reporting runs on a clock. [#early] NIS2 requires an early warning within 24 hours of becoming aware. [#note] An incident notification follows within 72 hours, [#final] and a final report within one month. [#dora2] DORA asks financial entities for an initial notification within 4 hours of classifying an incident as major.

## suppliers
Third, your suppliers' security is your duty. [#know] You are expected to know who they are, and to assess their risk. [#contract] Put obligations in the contract. [#exit] Under DORA, have an exit plan for the ones you cannot lose. [#excuse] Blaming the supplier is an explanation, not an excuse.

## prove
Fourth, the rules ask for proof. [#policy] A policy document is not evidence. [#train] NIS2 requires board members to follow cybersecurity training. [#test] Testing shows the measures work. Does the backup restore? Does the incident process hold? [#tl] Financial entities face the strongest version under DORA.

## good {hold=1.2}
What good looks like. [#own] One named executive, accountable to the board. [#frame] A written, dated, board-approved risk framework. [#drill] A reporting drill against the clock. [#reg] A supplier register, with contract clauses and exit plans. [#ev] And evidence: minutes, approvals, test reports and training records.

## first {hold=1.2}
A suggested first step. [#q1] Ask who in this room owns it, by name. [#q2] Then ask when the board last saw evidence, not assurances. [#legal] Have legal confirm which regime applies.

## outro {hold=2}
Compliance is the floor, not the goal. [#keep] Name the owner, and ask for evidence.
