# The first 72 hours. Four ways they go wrong.

## intro {hold=1}
The alarm has gone off at three in the morning. [#who] Who decides what happens next? [#four] The first 72 hours go wrong in four ways.

## decide
First, nobody knows who decides. [#sun] It is Sunday, three in the morning, and the on-call engineer sees the encryption spreading. [#off] Switching off the network would also stop the factory and the webshop. [#nobody] Is that the engineer's call, or the CEO's? Every minute spent finding out, the incident grows.

## evidence
Second, the evidence is gone within the hour. [#reboot] The instinct is to reboot the server and wipe the laptop. [#lost] That destroys the memory, the logs and the timestamps. [#claim] Then we cannot say what was taken, and the insurer may refuse the claim.

## comms
Third, five audiences hear five different stories. [#five] Staff, customers, press, the regulator and the insurer. [#contra] Someone says no data was affected before anyone knows. [#clock] The legal clocks started when we became aware: a 24-hour early warning under the {NIS2|niss two} directive, and 72 hours under {GDPR|G D P R}.

## help
Fourth, day one is spent looking for help. [#firms] Forensic firms are fully booked, because everyone is calling at once. [#proc] Without a retainer, day one becomes a procurement exercise, with the attacker still inside.

## good {hold=1.2}
What good looks like. [#plan] A response plan with written decision rights and named deputies. [#phone] A printed phone list. [#isolate] Isolate, don't wipe. [#tmpl] Pre-approved templates, and a deadline list. [#ret] A retainer signed in peacetime. [#oob] An out-of-band channel, and a yearly tabletop exercise. [#art] {NIS2|niss two} Article 23 sets the clocks.

## first {hold=1.2}
A suggested first step. [#ask] Ask two questions. [#q1] When did this board last rehearse an incident? [#q2] Is the phone list printed somewhere that is not on the network?

## outro {hold=2}
Nothing on this list is technology. [#keep] It is paper, phone numbers and one rehearsal a year.
