The CRA covers every product with digital elements: hardware or software that can connect to a device or a network, sold in the EU, including free software with a commercial purpose. You are the manufacturer when you sell it under your own name, even if someone else built it. An importer or reseller that puts its own brand on a product, or changes it substantially, becomes the manufacturer too. Medical devices, cars and aircraft follow their own rules and are out of scope. Most products fall in the default class and assess themselves. Annex III names important products: class I (such as password managers, VPNs, operating systems, routers, smart locks and connected toys) and class II (such as firewalls and hypervisors). Annex IV names critical products (such as smart meter gateways and smartcards).

Business impact: the first step is a list: every product you sell, whether the CRA applies, and which class it falls in. The class decides whether you may assess yourself or need an outside assessor, and that decides lead time and cost. The Commission's technical descriptions (November 2025) and its guidance of July 2026 (non-binding) help with the edge cases, such as remote data processing and open source.

The reporting duty (Art. 14) is already in force, and it also covers products you sold before December 2027. When you become aware that a vulnerability in your product is being actively exploited, or of a severe incident affecting its security, the CRA requires an early warning within 24 hours, a notification within 72 hours, and a final report within 14 days after a fix is available (one month for an incident). Everything goes once, through the Single Reporting Platform run by ENISA, to the CSIRT of the country where you are based. In the Netherlands that is the NCSC. Informing your customers about the fix and what they can do is part of the duty.

Business impact: twenty-four hours is shorter than most internal escalation paths. Reporting late can be fined up to €15 million or 2.5% of worldwide turnover, whichever is higher (Art. 64). The exercise worth doing: one named person who can file, an account on the platform, and a one-page template, rehearsed once.

De meldplicht (art. 14) is al van kracht en geldt ook voor producten die u vóór december 2027 verkocht. Alles gaat in één keer, via het centrale meldingsplatform van ENISA, naar het CSIRT van het land waar u gevestigd bent. In Nederland is dat het NCSC. From 11 December 2027 every product placed on the market, and every existing product that is substantially modified, meets the essential requirements of Annex I. In plain terms: shipped without known exploitable vulnerabilities and with secure defaults, a cybersecurity risk assessment, an SBOM of at least the top-level components, a way for researchers to report vulnerabilities, security updates for a support period of at least five years (or the expected lifetime if shorter), and the end date of support shown at purchase. The manufacturer runs a conformity assessment, draws up technical documentation and the EU declaration of conformity, and affixes the CE mark. Default products assess themselves. Class I may do so only when harmonised standards are fully applied. Class II and critical products need a notified body or an EU certification.

Business impact: this is a product roadmap item with a fixed date. Fourteen months is short for a hardware product with a notified body in the chain, and the Commission expects the first harmonised standards only in late 2026, with more in October 2027. Consider starting with the SBOM and the vulnerability process: they are the quickest wins, and they also answer what your NIS2 and DORA customers ask today. The support period is a cost that belongs in the price.

Vanaf 11 december 2027 voldoet elk product dat in de handel wordt gebracht, en elk bestaand product dat wezenlijk wordt gewijzigd, aan de essentiële eisen van bijlage I. If you bring a product into the EU, you are the importer. Before you sell, you check that the manufacturer has done the conformity assessment and the technical documentation, that the product carries the CE mark, the declaration of conformity and the user information, and you put your own contact details on it. If you only resell, you are the distributor: you act with due care, verify the CE mark, and check that manufacturer and importer did their part. Both pass on any vulnerability they learn of to the manufacturer without delay, warn the authorities when the risk is significant, and inform users when the manufacturer stops operating. Importers keep the declaration for at least ten years. And if you sell it under your own brand, or change it substantially, you carry the manufacturer's duties yourself.

Business impact: for a reseller this is mostly paperwork and one decision: which products do we still carry when the mark is missing? For a white-label business it is the full set of obligations in step 03. Fines for importer and distributor duties go up to €10 million or 2% of worldwide turnover.

In the Netherlands via the Cyberbeveiligingswet, in force since 15 August 2026.Your NIS2 customers assess their suppliers. Expect them to ask for your EU declaration, SBOM and support period. A CRA-conformant product is the shortest answer.An exploited vulnerability in your product can count as a significant incident for your own organisation if it meets the thresholds: early warning within 24 hours of becoming aware, incident notification within 72 hours, final report within one month. Two clocks, one incident.
DORADORA binds financial entities, not product makers. If you supply ICT to a bank or insurer, you are an ICT third-party provider in their register, with contract clauses on vulnerabilities and updates. DORA has applied since 17 January 2025.Financial customers write the right to information on vulnerabilities and updates into the contract. Your CRA reporting and support period give you the content.Your customer classifies: initial notification within 4 hours of classifying it as major (at most 24 hours after becoming aware), intermediate report within 72 hours of the initial notification, final report within one month. Your CRA notification feeds theirs.
SOC 2Not a legal duty. Criteria CC7.1 (identifying vulnerabilities), CC7.4 (responding to incidents) and CC8.1 (change management) map onto the CRA's vulnerability handling and reporting.A supplier's SOC 2 report shows whether the processes exist and were tested. It does not show CRA conformity; ask for the EU declaration separately.A missed or late CRA report that is also a security incident will likely appear as an exception in the next report.
ISAE 3402The focus is financial reporting. The CRA appears only where product security affects controls your customers rely on.The report rarely covers product security. Ask for the EU declaration and the support period in the contract instead.ISAE 3402 creates no duty to notify user auditors. Expect a deviation in the next report, and ask for interim disclosure in the contract.

NIS2NIS2 gaat over de organisatie, de CRA over het product. Bent u ook een essentiële of belangrijke entiteit, dan vereist NIS2 beveiliging van de toeleveringsketen en omgang met kwetsbaarheden (art. 21); uw CRA-processen dienen beide. In Nederland via de Cyberbeveiligingswet, van kracht sinds 15 augustus 2026.Uw NIS2-klanten beoordelen hun leveranciers. Verwacht vragen naar uw EU-verklaring, SBOM en ondersteuningsperiode. Een CRA-conform product is het kortste antwoord.Een misbruikte kwetsbaarheid in uw product kan voor uw eigen organisatie als significant incident gelden als het de drempels haalt: vroegtijdige waarschuwing binnen 24 uur na ontdekking, incidentmelding binnen 72 uur, eindverslag binnen een maand. Twee klokken, één incident.
DORADORA bindt financiële instellingen, geen productmakers. Levert u ICT aan een bank of verzekeraar, dan bent u een ICT-derde in hun register, met contractbepalingen over kwetsbaarheden en updates. DORA geldt sinds 17 januari 2025.Financiële klanten leggen het recht op informatie over kwetsbaarheden en updates vast in het contract. Uw CRA-meldingen en ondersteuningsperiode leveren de inhoud.Uw klant classificeert: eerste melding binnen 4 uur na classificatie als ernstig (uiterlijk 24 uur na ontdekking), tussenrapport binnen 72 uur na de eerste melding, eindrapport binnen een maand. Uw CRA-melding voedt die van hen.
SOC 2Geen wettelijke plicht. Het toont geen CRA-conformiteit; vraag apart naar de EU-verklaring.Een gemiste of te late CRA-melding die ook een beveiligingsincident is, komt waarschijnlijk als afwijking in het volgende rapport.
ISAE 3402De focus ligt op de financiële verslaggeving. Vraag in het contract naar de EU-verklaring en de ondersteuningsperiode.ISAE 3402 schept geen meldplicht aan de auditors van gebruikers.
